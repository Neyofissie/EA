Strategy rules · Take Profit Carlos (5M)
Trendline (3+ bounces) breaks → BOS → institutional push (3+ same-colour or 2 big candles) → first FVG of the push makes the zone (FVG to the swing). Enter when price comes back and completely fills the FVG, stop just past the swing, 1:2 minimum, aim 1:3–1:5. Only inside the session kill zone, only when the 13 EMA is clean.
Opening-range breakout (second strategy)
Pick it in the Strategy menu at the top. The range is the first N minutes after the open; the trade is the first candle that closes outside it. One trade a day, flat by the close.

Build keys into the app
Saves you typing keys on every phone and computer. Press the button, put the downloaded config.js into the app folder next to index.html, and upload the folder to Netlify again. Anyone who opens your site can read keys built into it. Only do this if you keep the link private. If a key leaks, make a new one (Twelve Data dashboard / @BotFather /revoke). Alerts
The rate is the share of earlier setups on the same pair, timeframe and settings that reached the target (default: 2× the risk, before touching the stop). It is shown on the chart's % badge, which turns yellow when a setup meets your rule. It is history, not a guarantee; no setup is ever sure.
